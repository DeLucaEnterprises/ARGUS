1 · Upload photos or video

Photos (JPG) and video (MP4 / MOV) go straight into data\input\ — the folder ARGUS analyses. Video is turned into 1 frame per second in the background, so the pipeline can analyse it like photos. No copying files by hand, no terminal.

🖼 USE DEMO PHOTOS — no photos of your own? This copies the demo photos shipped with ARGUS (in sample-data\, downloaded with the GitHub repo) into data\input\, so you can hit Run straight away. The set is 3 drone photos: DJI_0066 (a small person, upper right), DJI_0065 (woods, no obvious person) and DJI_0055 (open field by a river, no obvious person) — a good first test is the brief adult person, dark shirt.

3 · Map

Grey pins: photos — click one to open its source photo. Coloured pins: ranked candidates — green ≥ 0.7, amber 0.4–0.7, red < 0.4 — click one for its crop and its source photo. Use the layer button (top right of the map) for Street, Satellite, or Topo, and the Fullscreen button for a full-screen map. Candidates from video frames have no GPS and do not appear here.

4 · Review

Ranked by Muse score, top first. Open a card, check the crop against the score and reason, then mark it Accept or Reject. Your calls are saved in data\output\review_decisions.json and survive a restart. Click a crop to see its source photo.

5 · Review Agent

Small, verifiable, on top of the ranked list — it proposes, you decide. It dedupes the same object in overlapping tiles, re-checks borderline Muse scores (0.4–0.6) with one tighter second pass, and drafts a one-line-per-candidate summary with a citation to tile, crop, SAM confidence and Muse score. No chat agent, no pipeline rewrite. Run it after the pipeline, then Accept / Reject in section 4 as usual.
